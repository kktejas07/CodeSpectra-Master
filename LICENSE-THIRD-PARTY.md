## Animated icons by Sadjad

- Source: animated Material-style icon set by Sadjad (Lottie / After Effects),
  purchased by the project owner under the standard licence.
- Allowed: use inside this application and its website.
- Not allowed: redistributing the set, or any icon on its own, outside this
  product. Do not copy these files into other projects.
- Attribution: "Animated icons by Sadjad" is shown on the public `/credits`
  page, linked from the site footer.
- Only the icons the app renders are in this repository, minified, under
  `frontend/public/animated-icons/` (built by
  `frontend/scripts/build-animated-icons.mjs`). The full purchased set is kept
  outside the repository and must never be committed.
