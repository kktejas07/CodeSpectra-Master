# Guardian Reports Guide

Learners can share a short weekly progress report with a parent or guardian. Set it up in **Settings → Guardians** (`/dashboard/settings/guardians`).

## Invite a guardian (learner)

1. Add up to **two** guardians: name, relation (parent, guardian or other), email and optionally a WhatsApp number.
2. Choose what is shared: practice time, tests taken, score trend, streak, study plan progress and placement drives. Everything is on by default; untick anything you want to keep private.
3. The guardian gets an invitation. You can change what is shared or **revoke** a guardian at any time.

## Accept the invitation (guardian)

Open the link in the email (`/guardian/accept`) and choose how to get the report: email, WhatsApp or both. If the learner is a minor, you also confirm you are the parent or legal guardian and enter a 6-digit code sent to your WhatsApp.

## The weekly report

Once a week the guardian gets a summary of the shared items: how much the learner practised, tests taken and how scores are moving, the streak, study plan completion and placement drive updates. Reports carry totals and trends only — **never** answers, question text, proctoring photos, integrity flags or scores that have not been released.

Every email has an unsubscribe link (`/guardian/unsubscribe`), and WhatsApp reports stop when the guardian replies STOP.
