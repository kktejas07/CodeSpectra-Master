# Hiring Pipeline Guide

The hiring pipeline (applicant tracking) lives under **Hiring** in the sidebar (`/dashboard/hiring`). Every organisation has its own jobs, candidates and pipeline; people only see what their hiring role allows.

## Jobs

Open **Hiring → Jobs** to create a job. Give it a title, description, requirements, location, work mode, experience range and skills. A new job starts as a **draft** that candidates cannot see.

Job statuses:
- **Draft** — being prepared, not visible to candidates
- **Open** — accepting applications (turn on the public apply page to share a link)
- **On hold** — paused
- **Closed** / **Filled** — no new applications

A job with applications cannot be deleted; close it instead so the history is kept.

## Pipeline stages

Each job has its own pipeline. The default is: Applied → Screening → Assessment → Bot screening → Virtual interview → Face-to-face interview → Client review → Offer → Hired (plus Rejected). You can add an HR round and a background check (BGV) stage, rename stages and remove the ones you do not use.

For every stage you choose:
- **Advance** — move candidates on automatically when they pass, or wait for a recruiter to approve the suggested move
- **On fail** — reject, put on hold, or ask a recruiter to review
- **SLA days** — candidates stuck longer than this are rejected automatically

Entering a stage can contact the candidate: an assessment stage sends the test invite, a bot-screening stage starts the WhatsApp or phone screening, an interview stage asks the candidate to pick a slot.

## Screening and match score

When a résumé arrives, CodeSpectra reads it and scores it against the job's screening criteria (must-have and nice-to-have skills, keywords, years of experience, location, notice period, education, expected salary). The **match score** (0–100) and the reasons behind it show on every application. Candidates at or above the pass score are shortlisted; missing a must-have can fail the screen when you require it.

## Moving candidates

Open a job's board to drag candidates between stages, or use **Move**, **Reject**, **Put on hold** and **Approve suggested move** on an application. Every move is recorded in the application's history with who did it and why.

## Interviews

Interview stages create an interview automatically. Recruiters can schedule or reschedule it (Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, the built-in video room, or a manual link) and change the panel; candidates can pick a slot themselves when self-scheduling is on. Interviewers fill a scorecard within 48 hours; the stage's pass rule (all yes, majority yes or average score) decides the outcome, and an AI summary of the panel's scorecards is shown to staff.

## HR round

The HR round sends the candidate a form to confirm their current and expected compensation. A recruiter then records the decision — **pass**, **fail** or **hold** — with notes and the offered CTC. Passing without the candidate's form needs an override reason. Compensation is visible to admins and recruiters only.

## Analytics

**Hiring → Analytics** shows the funnel per stage (how many reached each stage, conversion, median days in stage), time to hire, where candidates came from and why they were rejected — for one job or all jobs.

## Using the AI copilot

Admins can ask the copilot to list jobs, find applicants with a skill, move or reject candidates, shortlist everyone above a match score, schedule interviews, start a screening or record an HR decision. The copilot only drafts these changes; nothing happens until you confirm the draft.
