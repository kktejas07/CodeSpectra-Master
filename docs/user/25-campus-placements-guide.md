# Campus Placements Guide

The campus placement portal connects a college's placement office with its students. Students use **Campus** (`/dashboard/campus`); the placement team uses the **Placement console** (`/dashboard/placement`).

## For students

- **Join your college** with the invite link from your placement office, or with your college email address. The placement office verifies you before you can apply.
- See the college's **placement drives** with the eligibility rules (department, batch, CGPA, backlogs). Drives you are not eligible for say why.
- **Apply** to a drive and track each application through its rounds.
- See your **offers** (company, CTC, joining date) and accept or decline them. Only you and the placement office see your offers.

## For the placement team

The organisation admin of a college sets the organisation up as a college (from Campus) and adds **placement officers** (existing accounts). The console has these tabs:

- **Overview** — placement statistics per graduation year: students placed, offers and median CTC (each placed student counts once, by their best live offer).
- **Students** — invite students in bulk with a CSV (`name, email, roll_no, department, batch, cgpa, backlogs`; only name and email are required), verify join requests, and manage departments and batches.
- **Readiness** — a 0–100 readiness score per student from recent company test practice (45 %), adaptive tests (30 %), contests (15 %) and skill passport level (10 %), with weak areas and top performers per department and batch. Students with no data show as inactive. Export it as CSV.
- **Drives** — create drives with eligibility and a process, shortlist applicants, record offers. A drive can run its test as a **live proctored drive** (see that guide), whose results come back as the shortlist.
- **Settings** — college details and placement staff.

## Privacy

Placement statistics and other students' records are visible only to the placement team. The AI copilot can list drives and batch readiness for college admins, inside their own college only.
