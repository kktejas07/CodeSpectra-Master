# Certifications Guide

CodeSpectra provides open-source certification modules to validate your skills. Access them from the sidebar (`/dashboard/certifications`).

## Browse the Catalog

Certifications are organized into two categories:

### Skill-Based Assessments
Languages and frameworks — e.g., JavaScript Basic, Python Basic, React.

### Role-Based Assessments
Job role-specific certifications covering broader skill sets.

Each certification card shows:
- **Icon** — visual identifier for the skill
- **Title** — certification name
- **Level** — Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced
- **Description** — what the certification covers
- **Duration** — estimated time to complete (in minutes)
- **Question count** — number of questions in the assessment
- **Passing score** — minimum percentage required to pass
- **Source** — attribution and license information (MDN, FreeCodeCamp, exercism, OSSU)

## Start an Assessment

1. Browse the catalog and find a certification you want to earn
2. Click **Start assessment** on the certification card
3. Begin the MCQ (multiple choice question) assessment at `/dashboard/certifications/[slug]`

### Assessment Format
- All assessments are **multiple choice question (MCQ)** format
- Each question has a time limit
- You can navigate between questions
- Submit all answers when finished

## Scoring and Passing

- **Passing score** is displayed on each certification card (typically 70-80%)
- Results are calculated immediately after submission
- If you pass, the certificate is generated and a badge is awarded
- If you don't pass, you can retake the assessment

### Track Progress
The certification page shows your earned count: "X/Y skills earned".

## View Your Certificates

After passing an assessment:
1. The certification card shows a green checkmark
2. Click **View certificate** to see your earned certificate
3. The certificate displays your name, the certification title, and your score

### Shareable Verification Links
Each certificate includes a unique verification token. Share the verification URL (`/cert/verify/[token]`) with recruiters or employers so they can confirm your credential in one click.

## Earning Badges
Passing certifications also awards achievement badges visible on your profile and the Achievements page (`/dashboard/achievements`).

## Benefits

- **Stand out** — earn shareable, verifiable certificates backed by open-source curricula
- **Open content** — every module attributes its source and license (no proprietary lock-in)
- **Verifiable** — recruiters can verify your certificate instantly
