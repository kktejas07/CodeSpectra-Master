# CodeSpectra User Handbook

Welcome to the CodeSpectra User Handbook! This is your official guide to everything you need to know about the platform. We built CodeSpectra to be your all-in-one ecosystem for coding assessments, skill development, and AI-powered pair programming.

This guide will walk you through the key features of the platform and how to leverage them for your career or organization.

---

## 1. The CodeSpectra Dashboard

Your journey starts at the Dashboard. Here, you'll find an overview of your progress, recent activity, and quick links to the platform's core modules.

- **Activity Heatmap**: Track your daily coding streaks and submission consistency.
- **Skill Radar**: A visual representation of your proficiencies across languages (e.g., Python, JavaScript) and domains (e.g., Algorithms, Frontend, Data Structures).
- **Recent Badges & XP**: See your gamified progress as you level up by completing challenges.

## 2. Technical Assessments & Challenges

Whether you're practicing for an interview or taking an official assessment for a job application, our assessment engine provides a seamless experience.

### Solving Problems
- **Browser-Based IDE**: Write code directly in the browser with full syntax highlighting, autocomplete, and a responsive layout.
- **Real-Time Execution**: Run your code against sample test cases instantly. Our secure execution sandbox supports over 15 programming languages (including Python, JS, Java, C++, Go, and Rust).
- **Difficulty Tiers**:
  - *Easy* (10 XP)
  - *Medium* (25 XP)
  - *Hard* (50 XP)
- **First-Blood Bonus**: Be the first to solve a newly released problem to earn a +25 XP bonus!

### AI-Powered Assistance
Stuck on a tricky problem? Click **"Ask CodeSpectra"** for progressive help:
1. **Nudge**: A gentle hint pointing you in the right direction.
2. **Approach**: A high-level strategy for solving the problem.
3. **Pseudo-code**: A step-by-step logical breakdown without giving away the exact syntax.
4. **Solution**: A fully explained, optimal solution (Note: requesting the full solution may reduce your XP reward).

## 3. Code Quality & Scanning (For Organizations)

For teams and enterprises, CodeSpectra offers powerful static analysis tools to ensure your codebase remains secure and maintainable.

- **Quality Ratings**: Code is graded on an A–E scale across Security, Reliability, Maintainability, and Technical Debt.
- **Quality Gates**: Projects can be configured to enforce strict rules (e.g., OWASP Top 10, NIST SSDF) before code can be merged.
- **Automated Fixes**: Our AI agents don't just find bugs—they suggest actionable fixes that you can apply with one click.

## 4. Certifications & Skill Tracks

Validate your expertise by earning official CodeSpectra Certifications.

- **Curated Tracks**: Follow structured learning paths for Frontend Development, Backend Engineering, Data Science, and more.
- **Verifiable Certificates**: Upon passing a certification exam, you'll receive a cryptographically verifiable certificate (with a unique URL) that you can easily share on LinkedIn or attach to your resume.
- **The Arena**: Participate in real-time coding competitions, hackathons, and codeathons to benchmark your skills globally.

## 5. Security & Privacy

We take your data and privacy seriously. During official, proctored assessments (if required by an employer), you may encounter our security suite:

- **Identity Verification**: Secure, opt-in Aadhaar ID and liveness checks to verify your identity.
- **Fair Play Monitoring**: We monitor tab switches, copy-pasting, and AI misuse to ensure a level playing field for all candidates.
- **Privacy First**: All proctoring data is strictly scoped to the specific assessment and securely encrypted. Review our Privacy Policy for more details.

## 6. Getting Help

Need assistance? 
- **AI Chatbot**: Available 24/7 in your IDE window for coding help.
- **Support Tickets**: Open a ticket via the `/dashboard/support` page if you encounter billing or account issues.
- **Community**: Join our open-source forums and discussions attached to specific coding problems to learn from fellow developers.
