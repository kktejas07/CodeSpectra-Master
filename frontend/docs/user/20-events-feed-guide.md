# Events Feed Guide

The events feed (`/events`) lists hackathons, coding contests, CTFs and buildathons from other organisers, gathered from public sources and partners.

## Finding events

Filter by kind, online or in person, city, theme, dates and prize. Open an event for its details and the organiser's registration link. You can save events to get a reminder before registration closes.

## Alerts and digests

Subscribe (`/subscriptions`) to get a weekly email digest of new events that match your filters, and optional WhatsApp alerts for new matching events. Email subscriptions are confirmed by email; you can change filters or unsubscribe from any message. WhatsApp alerts respect quiet hours.

## Moderation (platform admins)

**Admin → Events feed** shows every source (enable, disable or add a feed) and every event. Admins can edit an event, **feature** it, **hide** spam, or **archive** it; edited fields are kept when the source refreshes. The AI copilot can search the feed, show subscriber numbers and draft feature / hide / archive actions.
