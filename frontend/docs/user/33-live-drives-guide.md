# Live Proctored Drives Guide

A live drive is one timed, proctored sitting of a company test for many candidates at once — a campus placement test run by a college, or a hiring test run by a company. Organisers and invigilators work from **Live drives** (`/dashboard/live-drives`).

## For candidates

1. You get an invitation with the date and time. Sign in with the email address the organiser invited.
2. The **lobby** opens a few minutes before the start. Run the **system check** (camera, microphone, network, browser).
3. Everyone starts at the same time. The server keeps your clock; your answers save automatically.
4. If your connection drops, sign in again — you can resume within the reconnect window and your time is kept.
5. Invigilators may send you warnings or broadcast messages. Do not switch tabs or leave the camera view; integrity signals are recorded.

## For organisers

- **Create a drive** for your college (placement team) or company (hiring team): pick the company test pattern and level, the schedule and the rules (lobby time, reconnect window, proctoring).
- **Import the roster** (candidate list) and add **invigilators**, each with their own group.
- The question paper is frozen when the drive is scheduled, so question bank refreshes never change it.
- After the drive, score and **publish results** to the linked campus drive (as the shortlist) or hiring job.

## For invigilators

The **control room** shows a live grid of your candidates with their status and integrity flags. You can warn a candidate, add time, pause and resume, terminate an attempt or broadcast a message. Every action is recorded in the drive's audit trail.

The AI copilot can list your organisation's live drives and their status.
