# Paid Certifications Guide

Paid certifications (`/certifications`) are proctored CodeSpectra exams that issue a verifiable credential. They are separate from the free certification modules in the dashboard.

## Earn a certification

1. Open a programme to see its sections, level (foundation, associate, practitioner or professional), pass mark, price and attempt rules.
2. **Buy an attempt** (INR through PhonePe or USD through Stripe).
3. Take the exam with proctoring on. Questions are drawn from the approved question bank for each section and do not repeat on a retake.
4. When you pass and your proctoring trust score is high enough, the credential is issued. Some programmes, or attempts below the trust threshold, go to a **human review** first; you are emailed the outcome.

Attempts have a cooldown between tries and a yearly limit.

## Your credential

Credentials appear in your skill passport with a public **verify page** (`/verify/<credential id>`) and an "Add to LinkedIn" button. Each credential has an expiry; you can renew from a set number of days before it expires by passing again.

## For platform admins

**Admin → Pro certifications** creates programmes (from a template or from scratch), checks question bank coverage for every section, sets prices and publishes. The **review queue** lists attempts waiting for a decision with score, trust score and proctoring flags; passing issues the credential, rejecting notifies the candidate. Credentials can be revoked with a reason. The AI copilot can list the review queue and draft pass / reject decisions for you to confirm.
