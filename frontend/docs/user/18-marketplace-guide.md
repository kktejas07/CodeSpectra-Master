# Marketplace Guide

The marketplace (`/marketplace`) sells digital downloads, physical items and services (for example 1:1 mock interviews) from approved sellers.

## Buying

- Browse or search the store, filter by category and price, and open a product to see its details, reviews and delivery information.
- Add items to your cart and check out with the payment methods shown for your country.
- Your orders are under **Dashboard → Orders**: download digital items (within the download limit), track shipping, or book the time slot of a service.
- To cancel or ask for a refund, open the order and choose the items. Refunds go back to the original payment method once approved.

## Selling

1. Apply under **Dashboard → Seller → Apply** with your store details, tax information and payout method (bank, UPI or Stripe Connect).
2. A platform admin reviews the application. Once approved you can add products.
3. New products go to **pending review**; an admin approves them before they appear in the store, or sends them back with a note.
4. Earnings become available after the refund window, then you can request a payout. Payout details are stored encrypted and only the last digits are ever shown.

## For platform admins

**Admin → Marketplace** has the moderation queues: seller applications, products waiting for review, refund requests, payout requests and sales reports. The AI copilot can list these and draft approvals, refund decisions and payout decisions; money decisions always need your confirmation.
