# WhatsApp Practice Guide

WhatsApp practice sends you one practice question a day on WhatsApp and grades your reply at once. Set it up in **Settings → WhatsApp practice** (`/dashboard/settings/whatsapp-practice`).

## Turn it on (double opt-in)

1. In Settings, enter your WhatsApp number, pick your domain and level (easy, medium or hard), your language and the hour you want questions, and tick the consent box.
2. We send a confirmation message to that number. Reply **Yes, join** (or type JOIN) from that phone. Nothing is sent until you confirm from the phone itself.

## Every day

- At your chosen hour (never during quiet hours, 9 pm to 8 am by default) you get one question from the approved question bank for your domain and level.
- Answer with the buttons or the list, or type your answer. You get the result and an explanation straight away.
- Correct answers earn XP and every answer counts for your app streak.
- You can ask for more with NEXT, up to your daily limit (3 questions a day by default).
- Once a week you get a five-question **mini mock** with a link to your study plan.

## Commands

| Send | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| `STOP` | Stops all practice messages at once |
| `START` | Starts them again |
| `NEXT` | Another question (within your daily limit) |
| `MOCK` | The weekly mini mock now |
| `LEVEL easy` / `medium` / `hard` | Changes your level |
| `TOPIC <subject>` | Switches your practice domain (for example `TOPIC sql`); `TOPIC` alone shows a list |
| `LANG en` / `hi` / `te` | Changes the language (English, Hindi or Telugu) |
| `HELP` | Lists the commands |

You can also turn it off in Settings at any time. We never log your phone number, answers or message text.
