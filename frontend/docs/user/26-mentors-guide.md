# Mentors Guide

The mentor marketplace (`/mentors`) lets learners book one-to-one sessions — career guidance, mock interviews and code reviews — with verified mentors.

## Book a mentor

1. Browse `/mentors`; filter by skill, company, language and price.
2. Open a mentor to see their profile, offerings, reviews and open slots (shown in your time zone).
3. Pick an offering and a slot and pay at checkout. You get an email with a calendar invite.
4. Join the session from **Sessions** (`/dashboard/sessions`) with the built-in video room, or the meeting link the mentor gave.

You can **reschedule** within the mentor's reschedule policy, attach code for a code review, and rate the session afterwards. If something went wrong, open a **dispute** from the session; a platform admin decides it and any refund goes back through the payment gateway.

## Become a mentor

Mentors are approved marketplace sellers. From **Mentor studio** (`/dashboard/mentor`):

1. Finish seller onboarding (identity / KYC and payout details) if you have not.
2. Fill in your profile: headline, bio, skills, companies, years of experience, languages.
3. Set your **availability** once (weekly hours, time zone, buffer between sessions, maximum sessions per day); it applies to every offering.
4. Create **offerings** (session type, length, price). Prices are in rupees or dollars; the marketplace commission is shown before you save.
5. Submit for verification. A platform admin verifies, rejects (with a note) or later suspends a profile. You are listed on `/mentors` once you are verified, your seller account is approved and you have at least one live offering.

Payouts, refunds, reviews and reminders work like any marketplace order (see the marketplace guide).

## For platform admins

**Admin → Mentors** lists profiles by status with seller and KYC status. The AI copilot can list mentors waiting for verification and draft verify / reject / suspend / reinstate decisions for you to confirm.
