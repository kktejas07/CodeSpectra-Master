# Company Tests Guide

Company tests let you practise the hiring test of a specific company. Each company page (`/companies/<company>`) describes the test pattern; practice runs are at `/dashboard/company-tests`.

## Exam patterns

A pattern lists the company's test sections (for example quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal ability, coding), the number of questions, the time per section, negative marking and the cut-off. Patterns are summaries of commonly reported public information, **not official data** — a pattern shows whether an admin has verified it against the company's own page. Companies change their tests often.

## Practise a company test

1. Open **Company tests** and pick a company.
2. Choose a level: **Easy**, **Moderate** or **Tough**.
3. Sections run one after another, each with its own clock. The server keeps the time: an answer sent after a section's time is up does not count, and a section closes on its own if you leave.
4. At the end you get your score per section against the cut-off (with negative marking where the company uses it) and a review with the correct answers.

Questions come from the approved question bank. A weekly refresh swaps part of each section for unused questions, so retakes stay fresh; a refresh never changes a run in progress.

## Company tests in your study plan and placements

A study plan with a company goal adds timed company tests at a level that follows your progress. Colleges use company test results for batch readiness, and live proctored drives use the same patterns (see the campus placements and live drives guides).

## For platform admins

**Admin → Company patterns** edits patterns, marks them verified and builds the Easy / Moderate / Tough versions. Versions are filled from the question bank first; the AI question generator only fills a shortfall, within the admin's daily and monthly caps, and its questions wait in the bank as drafts until a reviewer approves them. The weekly refresh is off until an admin turns it on. The AI copilot can build a company test from a pattern as a draft.
