# Cohort Courses Guide

A cohort (`/dashboard/cohorts`) is a dated, live run of a course: a group of learners starts together, attends scheduled sessions with mentors and teaching assistants, hands in assignments and earns a completion certificate.

## For learners

- **Enrol** in an open cohort. Depending on the cohort, enrolment is free, included in your plan, covered by your college's or company's licence seat, or bought as a seat in the marketplace. A cohort closes when it is full.
- **Sessions** appear in your calendar (download the .ics). Join from the cohort page; joining marks your attendance.
- **Assignments** have deadlines; submit code (autograded where tests exist) or a file. Late work follows the cohort's late rule.
- Your **progress** shows attendance, assignment scores and what you still need for the certificate.
- Each cohort has a private community category for questions.

## For mentors and teaching assistants

Cohort staff run sessions, record attendance, create assignments with a rubric, and grade submissions from the **grading queue**.

## For organisation admins

Organisation admins (and platform admins) create cohorts for their organisation: pick the course, dates, time zone, capacity, access rules and a weekly schedule (sessions are generated from it), add staff, and set the completion rule (attendance and assignment score). When the cohort ends, **Issue certificates** gives a certificate to everyone who met the rule. The AI copilot can list your organisation's cohorts.
