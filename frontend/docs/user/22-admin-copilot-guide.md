# Admin AI Copilot Guide

The AI copilot (`/dashboard/admin/agent`) answers questions about your organisation's data and drafts changes for you. It works across tests, candidates, hiring jobs and applicants, hackathons, codeathons, the question bank, workflows and — for platform admins — the marketplace, Learn hub and events feed.

## Draft, then confirm

Reading is immediate. Anything that saves, sends, publishes, charges money or deletes comes back as a **draft card** with a preview. Nothing changes until you press **Confirm**; you can **Edit** the details in a form or **Cancel**. Drafts expire after 24 hours.

## How it works on a request

1. For bigger requests it shows a **plan** as a checklist and ticks steps off as it works.
2. It looks things up (several at once when they are independent) and may hand part of the job to a **specialist** (hiring, assessments, events, marketplace, content, automations, analytics) — with exactly your permissions.
3. If something is missing it **asks you a question**, often with quick answers to click.
4. Before answering it checks ids and claims against what the tools returned and lists its sources.

Multi-part setups (for example "set up a campus drive") come as one **bundle** of drafts. **Confirm all** runs the steps in order; if one fails, the rest do not run and the card shows what was done.

## Usage and limits

Each message is charged for the AI it actually uses (including questions or problems it generates for a draft), even if you stop it part-way. Your organisation has daily and monthly copilot budgets, and each admin has a message rate limit; open **Usage** next to the model name to see today's and this month's usage. Platform admins set the budgets there.
