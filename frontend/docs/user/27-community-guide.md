# Community Guide

The community forum (`/community`) is where learners ask questions, share solution write-ups and help each other. Signed-in members post from **Community** in the dashboard (`/dashboard/community`).

## Ask and answer

- Pick a **category** and start a thread with a clear title. Code blocks and links are allowed (new accounts can post only a few links at first).
- Reply, **vote** helpful posts up, and **follow** a thread to be notified of new replies.
- The person who asked can **accept** the answer that solved it.
- Problem pages show community **solution write-ups** once you have solved the problem yourself.

## Reputation

Accepted answers and upvotes earn reputation; reputation also gives XP (up to a daily limit).

## Study groups

**Groups** (`/dashboard/community/groups`) are small private spaces with a chat. Create a group, share its join code, and leave at any time. Cohort courses get their own private category automatically.

## Keeping it safe

Every post goes through the moderation pipeline: profanity is masked or rejected, spam patterns and links from new accounts are held for review, and (when the platform turns it on) an AI check runs on text that passed the prompt-injection scan. Use **Report** on any post; a post with several open reports is held for review until a moderator looks at it.

## For moderators

Platform admins appoint forum moderators. The **moderation queue** (`/dashboard/community/moderation`) shows held posts and open reports. A moderator can approve, hide, remove or restore a post, dismiss reports, lock or pin a thread, and ban an author for 1–365 days. The AI copilot can list the queue and draft these actions for a moderator to confirm. When the platform switches on the **community moderator** agent loop, it reports doubtful new posts into this queue (reporter: community moderator) and may hide clear spam; see the [agent loops guide](./agent-loops-guide.md).
