# CodeSpectra Participant Handbook

Welcome to the **CodeSpectra Participant Handbook**. This guide is designed to help you navigate the platform, solve problems efficiently in the workspace, and understand how your submissions are evaluated. 

> [!NOTE]
> This handbook is for participants and standard users. If you are a team administrator, please refer to the Admin Guide.

---

## 1. Getting Started: The Dashboard

When you log in, you will land on your **Dashboard**. This is your central hub for tracking progress.

*   **Active Events:** Any hackathons, exams, or codeathons you are currently enrolled in will appear at the top.
*   **Skill Radar & Stats:** Visualize your proficiency across different languages and domains.
*   **Recent Activity:** A log of your recent submissions and earned XP.

---

## 2. Participating in Hackathons & Events

When you join a Hackathon or Assessment, you will be taken to the **Event Details** page. 

### Choosing Your Problem
Depending on how the event is configured, you will face one of two scenarios:
1.  **Solve All:** You are expected to submit solutions for every problem listed.
2.  **Pick One (Solve Limit):** You can browse the descriptions of all available problems, but you are required to select and lock in **only one** problem to solve. Once you lock in a problem, you cannot change your choice.

### The Coding Workspace
Click **"Solve"** on a problem to enter the Workspace IDE.

*   **Problem Description (Left Panel):** Contains the problem statement, constraints, and example inputs/outputs.
*   **Code Editor (Center Panel):** A full-featured code editor. Select your preferred programming language from the dropdown at the top.
*   **Terminal & Output (Bottom Panel):** Where you can run your code against custom inputs and see the execution results.

### Running and Submitting Code
1.  **Run Code:** Click the **Run** button to compile and execute your code against the provided sample test cases. This helps you debug without using a formal attempt.
2.  **Submit:** When you are confident in your solution, click **Submit**. Your code will be evaluated against hidden test cases. 
3.  **Attempts & Penalties:** Be mindful of your submissions! Some events apply a point penalty for incorrect submissions or have a hard limit on the total number of attempts.

## 3. Using the AI Assistant

During standard practice (and permitted events), you have access to the CodeSpectra AI Assistant in your workspace.

*   **Ask for Hints:** Stuck on a bug? Ask the AI for a nudge.
*   **Code Review:** The AI can analyze your submitted code and suggest improvements for time and space complexity.
*   **Fair Play:** In strict assessments, the AI assistant may be disabled. Always adhere to the rules of your specific event.

---

## 4. Leaderboards and XP

As you solve problems on the platform, you will earn XP (Experience Points).

*   **Difficulty Scaling:** Harder problems yield more XP.
*   **Global Leaderboard:** Compare your standing with other developers worldwide on the `/dashboard/leaderboard` page.
*   **Event Leaderboards:** Hackathons and Codeathons have their own isolated leaderboards based on event-specific scoring rules (factoring in execution time, memory usage, and attempt penalties).

---

## 5. Support & Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues during an assessment:
1.  **Check your internet connection**, as the IDE requires a constant connection to compile code.
2.  **Ensure your browser is up to date.** We recommend the latest versions of Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
3.  **Contact Support:** Use the "Support" tab in the sidebar if you experience platform-breaking bugs.

*Happy Coding!*
