# Study Plans Guide

A study plan (`/dashboard/study-plan`) is a personal week-by-week schedule built from your own results. It tells you what to read, what to practise and which tests to take, and it changes every week as you make progress.

## Create a plan

Open **Study plan** in the sidebar and choose a goal:

- **Company** — prepare for one company's hiring test (for example a service company's aptitude test). The plan studies that company's test sections first and adds timed company practice tests.
- **Skills** — up to six skill areas (for example data structures, SQL, aptitude).

Then pick how many days you have (7 to 180) and how many hours a week you can study (2 to 40). The plan starts today in your time zone.

## How the plan is chosen

CodeSpectra reads your recent results — test answers by topic and difficulty, adaptive test levels, company test section scores, practice sets and AI mock interviews from the last 180 days — and works out your **weak areas** (lowest mastery first). Topics you have never been tested on get a starting priority, so a new learner still gets a full plan.

Each week holds a daily challenge, Learn articles for your weak topics, practice sets at your next difficulty level and tests. Near the end of the plan the mix shifts from reading to practice and full tests. The same results always give the same week; the AI only writes the short weekly coach note.

## Each week

- Tick items off as you finish them. Finished items earn XP and count for your streak.
- **Practice sets** open inside the plan and are graded at once; your answers feed the next week's weak areas.
- Every week the plan adapts: if you finished most items the load grows a little, if you fell behind it shrinks.
- **Rebuild** starts the plan again from your latest results (for example after a mock test).

## Reminders

Turn on email and/or WhatsApp reminders and choose the hour. WhatsApp reminders need WhatsApp practice consent (see the WhatsApp practice guide). Every email has an unsubscribe link.

## Guardians and colleges

If you share your study plan with a parent or guardian, their weekly report shows how much of the week you completed (see the guardian reports guide). Admins can look at a learner's plan from the admin copilot only inside their own organisation.
