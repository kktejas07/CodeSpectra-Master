# Interviews Guide

The AI-powered mock interview platform helps you practice for technical interviews. Access it from the sidebar (`/dashboard/interviews`).

## Available Interview Types

The platform offers different interview types:
- **Coding** — technical coding challenges
- **System Design** — architecture and design problems
- **Behavioral** — soft skills and experience questions
- **AI Fluency** — AI and machine learning concepts

Each interview type shows:
- Title and description
- Duration
- Difficulty level
- Roles it targets (e.g., Frontend, Backend, Full Stack)

Some interview types may be locked or "Coming Soon" — availability is indicated on each card.

## Setup Process

### Step 1: Role Selection
1. Click **Start Interview** on the main page
2. Select your target role from the role selector
3. Choose the interview type (technical, behavioral, or system design)

### Step 2: Resume Upload (Optional)
1. Upload your resume (PDF or text)
2. The AI uses your resume to tailor interview questions
3. Resume upload is optional but recommended for personalized questions

### Step 3: Audio/Camera Settings
1. Configure your **microphone** and **camera**
2. Test your audio input level
3. Preview your camera feed
4. Click Continue when ready

## Taking a Live Interview

### Dynamic Interview
After setup, you're taken to the interview workspace (`/dashboard/interviews/dynamic`):
- **Problem statement** displayed on screen
- **Code editor** for writing solutions
- **Video/proctoring** is active during the interview
- **Timer** shows remaining time

### During the Interview
1. Read the problem statement carefully
2. Write your solution in the code editor
3. Run code against provided test cases
4. Submit your solution when complete
5. The AI evaluates your submission in real-time

### AI-Powered Dynamic Interview
The interview adapts based on your performance:
- Questions adjust difficulty based on your answers
- Follow-up questions probe deeper into your reasoning
- The AI evaluates your approach, not just correctness

## Identity Verification

Some interviews require identity verification:
1. You'll be prompted to upload a selfie and ID photo
2. The AI (Gemini 3 Flash with vision) verifies your identity
3. You receive confirmation before the interview begins

## Viewing Feedback and Results

After completing an interview, results are available at `/dashboard/interviews/results`.

### Feedback Includes
- **Score** — overall performance rating
- **Per-question evaluation** — how you performed on each question
- **Code quality** — analysis of your solutions
- **Areas for improvement** — specific recommendations
- **Strengths** — what you did well

### Interview History
Your past interviews are listed with:
- Interview type and role
- Date completed
- Score
- Status (pending review, completed)

Click any past interview to view detailed feedback.
